Troy's File Renamer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short: Troy's File Renamer works entirely on your own computer. It does not collect, store, send or share any information about you or your files.

What the app does with your files

When you choose a drive or folder and press Scan, the app reads the names of the folders and video files it finds so it can suggest new file names. This happens only on your device. File names are shown to you in a preview, and a file is renamed only after you confirm.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet and does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.

Information stored on your device

The app does not save settings, history, or copies of your file names. The only change it makes to your files is the rename you approve.

Microsoft Store

If you obtained the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information about your download and use of the Store under its own privacy statement. That is separate from this app and outside the developer's control.

Children

The app is a general file-management utility and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.
